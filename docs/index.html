One tool.

tully lives in your menu bar and does the ten small things you do every day: watch the system, place windows, convert files, clean the disk. Nothing new to learn. It just works the way your Mac already does.

§ 01

The menu bar, read at a glance.

CPU, memory, disk and network next to the icon, in four styles. Fixed widths, so nothing jumps around. Orange above 65 %, red above 85 %.

01 / 06
§ 03

Drag a file. Tap a key.

Start dragging any file and tap a modifier: a wheel opens under the cursor. Drop on a segment and the result lands next to the original. No window to open, no permissions needed.

§ 06

Up and running in a minute.

  1. 01Download the latest tully.zip from GitHub Releases and move tully to Applications.
  2. 02The app isn't notarized: the first time, right-click → Open.
  3. 03Look for the ring in the menu bar. Grant Accessibility when you want the window features.
  4. 04Optional, for video, audio and WebP:brew install ffmpeg webp
